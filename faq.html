Frequently Asked Questions

Plain answers about what SHIELD CryptoGuard does today, and what it does not.

// 01 — WHAT SHIELD DOES
Q.01 What is SHIELD CryptoGuard and who operates it?
+

SHIELD CryptoGuard is a Chrome extension in public beta, operated by Marbella Collins LLC, a Florida (USA) limited liability company. It does three things: it checks sites for phishing, it warns you about unlimited token approvals, and (PRO) it checks token contracts with GoPlus Security.

Q.02 How does the phishing check work?
+

On every page you open, SHIELD compares the domain with a list of known phishing domains (a built-in list plus CryptoScamDB data, refreshed every 6 hours) and with a list of legitimate crypto sites, to catch look-alike names. If the domain matches, SHIELD shows a warning on the page and a browser notification. Suspicious URL patterns (such as “airdrop” or “claim reward”) appear as MEDIUM risk when you open the SHIELD popup. SHIELD only knows the domains and patterns on these lists.

Q.03 What does the unlimited approval warning do?
+

When a site asks your wallet to approve a very large (unlimited) amount of an ERC-20 token, SHIELD shows a warning on the page and suggests setting a spending limit in your wallet. It does not block the transaction: you decide.

Q.04 How does the honeypot check work? (PRO)
+

Paste a token contract address into the SHIELD popup. SHIELD asks the GoPlus Security token-security API (Ethereum mainnet) and shows what GoPlus reports: honeypot flag, sell tax above 10% (critical above 49%), hidden or reclaimable ownership, mintable supply, fewer than 50 holders. The result comes from GoPlus; SHIELD does not verify it independently.

Q.05 Which networks are covered?
+

Phishing checks and approval warnings work on any website, whatever network your wallet uses. The PRO contract check covers Ethereum mainnet only.

// 02 — PRIVACY & DATA
Q.06 Does SHIELD access my private keys or seed phrase?
+

No. SHIELD never asks for, reads or stores private keys or seed phrases. Anything claiming to be SHIELD and asking for your seed phrase is a scam.

Q.07 What data leaves my browser?
+

Phishing checks run in your browser; SHIELD downloads the CryptoScamDB list from GitHub. With PRO, the contract address you paste is sent to GoPlus Security. If you log in, your email is sent to our server to check your subscription. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

// 03 — PLANS

Q.10 Does SHIELD guarantee I will not lose funds?

No. SHIELD is in beta. It reduces, but does not eliminate, risk: it only knows the phishing domains on its lists, it only warns about very large approvals, and contract results depend on GoPlus. Always check before you sign.
